Peakopia Game Guide

As of v3.6.0 · Operated by melange lab

Peakopia is a stock-timing game played with virtual money. Tap once to buy, watch the chart, and tap again to sell. Sell before the crash and you keep a profit. Wait too long and you are stuck at the lower price. A round takes only a few seconds.

Good to know first
  • It is free. There is no install, no sign-up and no login. It opens right in your browser.
  • All money in the game is virtual. There are no purchases, and nothing can be cashed out.
  • Every company, stock, news item and chat message in the game is made up. None of them are real.
  • This game is not investment advice.

What kind of game is it?

When a round starts, the chart begins to rise. At some point a crash comes. In a normal round there is always a signal right before the crash: one volume bar under the chart suddenly shoots up. Sell as soon as you see it and you keep your profit. The signal lasts only about 0.5 seconds, so you need to keep your eyes on the chart.

News pops up during a round. Sometimes bad news comes out and the price drops at once. That is a fake drop meant to scare you into selling (a shakeout). Telling the real signal from the fake one is the skill this game is about.

As you play, XP builds up and your level and rank rise. Rising ranks open new stock types, items, leverage, gear and partners along the way. You can raise animal partners, buy gear and park spare cash in investment products. Every 10th round is a boss fight where no money is used.

Who is it for?

  • People who want to play a round or two on a short break
  • People who want to try terms like chart, volume and leverage without real money
  • People who enjoy growth games where your record builds up bit by bit

As you level up, ‘Investing notes’ unlock one by one. They briefly explain basics such as compound growth, diversification, volatility and bubbles, plus real history such as the 1929 crash and the dot-com bubble. Your results in the game have nothing to do with real investing, though. Doing well here does not mean you will do well with real money.

This game is not made mainly for children under 14.

The screen at a glance

You move between five tabs at the bottom. The (?) button on the Missions, Grow, Shop and Account tabs shows that tab’s rules.

TabWhat it is for
TradePick a stock, buy and sell. It has the chart, volume, the news bar, % of limit and the Items button.
MissionsDaily missions, weekly missions, career missions, the Daily Challenge, Friend League and Season Pass.
GrowSwitch partners, spend SP on skills and see the rank table.
ShopBuy consumables and gear, borrow or repay at the Bank, and decorate your Mini-room.
AccountSee your assets, investments, collection, records and achievements. Open Settings with ⚙ at the top right.

At the top of the screen are your level, the Daily Gift 🎁 and SP 🌳 buttons. Tap your level to open the ‘Level road’, which shows what opens at the next levels.

  • v3.2 · Every normal crash now comes after a signal. A few luck-based items and products were removed. Max Buy now uses all your cash, and loading a backup code became sturdier.
  • v3.1 · The Level road, which shows what each level opens, and the 10 ranks of Part 2 “Beyond the Market” from Lv.60 were added. The Daily Gift and treasure chests became fixed rewards that you can see in advance.

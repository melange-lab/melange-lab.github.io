Growth

Peakopia guide · as of v3.6.0

This page covers everything that builds up as you play: levels and ranks, skills, partners, gear, investments, collection, retirement, friend codes and backup codes. Everything you buy to grow is paid with virtual money only.

Levels and XP

XP raises your level. Lv.1 to Lv.2 takes 60 XP; after that, going from Lv.L to the next level takes 40 + 20 × L XP. For example, Lv.2 to Lv.3 takes 80 XP.

  • Take profit: roughly 8–48 XP (more for higher return and peak accuracy; doubled in Fever)
  • Caught: 3 XP
  • Boss defeat: 40–60 XP (less for a rematch)
  • Completing daily missions, the Daily Gift and career mission tiers

Every level raises your per-round Trade limit by 22%, and every 2 levels give 1 SP. 🏠 Downtown Studio gear and the 📚 Trade Review skill raise the XP you earn.

Level road

Tap your level at the top to open the Level road. It shows what each level opens: the next gear step, 30 Investing notes, room decor, new stock names and more. Lv.30 opens the chart theme picker, Lv.35 the emblem shelf, Lv.45 the window view picker, Lv.50 name frames and the investor card, and Lv.55 the music picker. What you open on the Level road stays when you retire.

Skills and SP 🌳

SP comes slowly: 1 per 2 levels, 1 at ★1 and 1 at ★5 per achievement (loan badges excluded), 1 per 2 career mission tiers, 1 per first defeat of a new rank Ⅰ–Ⅲ Boss Book entry or first ★★★, and 1 per completed weekly mission. Collection and retirement give SP too.

  • Each skill’s upgrade cost rises by 1 for every 5 levels in that skill.
  • The top skill of each branch opens once you put 10 points into that branch.
  • ‘Reset all skills’ on the Grow tab gives back all spent SP so you can choose again.

The 12 partners 🐾

Your first partner is the animal from your investor type quiz. If you skipped the quiz, it is the Squirrel. The other friends join when you reach a rank or an achievement. Tap a friend to make it your partner.

  • Bond grows by 1 every round, by 1 more for a take profit, and by 3 for a boss defeat. Practice and challenge rounds do not count.
  • Each friend keeps its own bond. At 0 it is a Baby, at 50 an Adult, and at 450 a Legend.
  • Only your current partner gives its effect. The effect grows from Baby to Adult to Legend.
  • Effects are always the same fixed numbers. There is no chance or draw involved.
  • If you switch partners mid-round, the change applies from the next round.

Gear D → SSS 🛒

Gear is bought in the Shop. Each of the 9 gear types goes up through 7 steps: D Common, C Fine, B Rare, A Epic, S Legend, SS Myth and SSS Divine. The first step (D) opens with rank, and C and above open with level. You go up one step at a time, at fixed prices.

  • Gear stays when you retire.
  • You cannot upgrade while in debt, and gear is seized in a debt bankruptcy.

Items and consumables

Items (🛡️ Stop-Loss Shield, 🔍 Hot Tip, ⏱️ Slow-Mo) are turned on each round, and you pay only for that round. Their effects are in Rules in detail.

Consumables are bought in the Shop and kept until you use them. The one on sale now is the 🔮 Crystal Ball. It marks next round’s crash point almost exactly, and it is not used on Max Buy rounds. Consumable prices scale with your cash, and each one you own makes the next 1.6× pricier. The 🎟️ Boss Summon Ticket is no longer sold.

Daily Gift and treasure chests

You can claim the 🎁 Daily Gift once a day. What each weekday gives is fixed and shown in advance. A 💎 Treasure Chest appears every 14 take profits, and its contents follow a fixed order. Neither is a random draw.

Investments 💼

On the Account tab you can put cash into investment products. While you play, they earn interest, or for products that swing, they can lose. Interest and swings are counted by play time, not by the number of rounds, and each round counts for at most 6 seconds.

  • Earning cap · Each product has a cap on the money that earns interest. Money above the cap earns nothing.
  • Patience · The average play time your money has stayed in. The longer it stays, the higher the rate and the cap. Each round you can add up to 10% of the balance without changing it, but new money beyond that starts again from 0 minutes. Taking everything out starts it over.
  • 10x+ or Max Buy · Every such round halves Patience on all products, and the earning caps shrink with it.
  • Withdrawal fee · Charged only on money that was earning interest.
  • While in debt · Investments up to the size of your debt (highest-rate products first) earn no interest and take no losses.
  • The 🐼 Panda partner adds +5/10/15% to the base interest of investment products.

Collection, asset ladder and Mini-room

  • 🏆 Collection · 50 items in 8 groups: Luxury, Rides, Real Estate, Sports, Art · Relics, Acquisitions, Space, and Myth · Legend. Each item you buy gives SP, and completing a group gives 3 bonus SP.
  • 🏛️ Asset ladder · 64 fixed-price steps from $1B up. There are 8 lines, and you buy one step at a time on each line. Each step costs 10 times the one before. You can buy only with cash and only with no debt. It gives no money or SP, changes nothing in play, and stays when you retire. The first line is open from the start, and a new line opens every 40 levels from Lv.50.
  • 📈 Total assets · Net worth plus the steps you have bought. Each time your lifetime best grows 10×, the result screen notes it (no money reward).
  • 🏠 Mini-room · As your total assets grow, you move into a better home. You can decorate it with wallpaper, floors and props from the Shop.

Retire and rebirth 🔁

Once your net worth passes the bar, you can retire on the Account tab and start a new run. The bar is $1B the first time and grows 10× with every retirement ($10B, $100B…).

  • Reset: cash, investments, consumables and debt
  • Kept: level, skills, gear, achievements, collection, the asset ladder and everything opened on the Level road
  • Permanent bonus for every retirement: take-profit gains +10% and SP +2
  • Round XP ×1.1 per retirement (up to ×3). Mission and gift XP stay the same.
  • You cannot retire in the middle of a round.

Missions, achievements and challenges

  • Missions · 8 daily missions change at midnight, and 10 weekly missions change every Monday. The 20 career missions set a bigger goal each time you reach one.
  • Achievements · Repeating an achievement raises it from ★1 to ★5.
  • Daily Challenge · Once a day, 5 rounds with the same crash points for everyone. Your money is not used. Your sell return is the score, and a crash costs 30 points.
  • Season Pass · XP from each round fills 30 reward tiers. A new season starts every Monday.

Friend codes

The game has no server. You compare with friends by swapping codes. A code is sent only through the app you choose, such as a messenger.

Friends’ Net Worth (SHGA- codes)

  1. Open ‘💰 Friends’ Net Worth’ in the friends section at the top of the Account tab.
  2. Tap 📤 My code and send it to a friend. The code looks like SHGA-date-net worth-level-nickname.
  3. Paste a code from a friend into the ➕ Friend code box. It joins the net-worth ranking.

Only the latest code per friend is kept, for up to 30 friends. You can remove one with ✕, and the list is included in your backup code.

Friend League (SHG- codes)

Daily Challenge scores are shared as codes that start with SHG-. Enter a friend’s SHG- code to rank by this week’s score.

Backup codes (SHG1:) 💾

Your progress is saved only in this device’s browser. Make a backup code in case you change devices or clear your browser data.

  1. Tap ‘💾 Copy Backup Code’ in Settings. A long text starting with SHG1: is copied.
  2. Paste it somewhere safe, such as a notes app.
  3. To load it, paste everything from SHG1: to the end into ‘📥 Load Backup’ in Settings. Your current progress is overwritten.
  • Extra text, line breaks and invisible characters around the code are ignored.
  • If the code was not copied to the end, the game tells you.
  • If one part looks broken, only that part goes back to its starting state and the rest loads.
  • Anyone with the code can load that progress, so keep it to yourself.

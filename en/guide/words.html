Glossary

Peakopia guide · as of v3.6.0

These are the words you see in the game, explained the way the game uses them. They can differ a little from their meaning in real markets. For exact numbers, see Rules in detail.

Trading and the chart

Buy · Sell
Buying and selling. In this game, one tap on the big button buys and the next tap sells.
Take profit
Selling above your buy price to keep the gain. Take profits in a row earn a streak bonus.
Caught
When the crash comes before you sell and you are stuck at the lower price. The loss is the fixed ‘Crash −N%’ share for that stock type.
Stop loss
Selling below your buy price to keep the loss small. You lose less than when caught, but your win streak ends.
Peakopia
The name of the English version. The Korean version is called 떡상 계좌 키우기. Either way, you keep a rise only if you sell before the crash.
Cash
Money you can use right now. Money parked in investment products is not part of your cash.
If you sell now
The profit or loss you would get by selling at this moment. While you hold, it keeps updating in the bar at the top.
Return
How much the price has risen from your buy price. If the price doubles, that is +100%.
Peak accuracy
How close to the highest price before the crash you sold. Missions and achievements use it often.
Volume
The bars under the chart, showing how much was traded at that moment. Big price moves often come with taller bars.
Volume signal
The signal that always comes right before a crash in a normal round. One volume bar suddenly shoots up to about 2–3 times its usual height. The crash follows after about 0.5 seconds by default.
Shakeout
A fake drop where the price falls at once with bad news. It is meant to scare you into selling, so at 1x you can hold. At 10x leverage or more, even this drop can liquidate you.
Good news · Bad news
News that is good for the price, or bad for it. In the news bar, good news is green, bad news is red and tips are purple.
Hot Tip
An item that shows the price range where the crash will come. It is always accurate and shows as two purple dashed lines on the chart.
Crystal Ball
A consumable that shows next round’s crash point as one near-exact line. It is not used on Max Buy rounds.

Risk and money

Leverage
Trading at a multiple of the amount you put in. Gains and losses grow by that multiple, higher multiples bring faster crashes, and fees cost more.
Liquidation line · Liquidated
With leverage, a liquidation line appears on the chart. If the price touches it, you are liquidated: you lose your whole trade amount and the round ends.
Max Buy
Buying with all your cash, ignoring % of limit. It is on for one round only, and if you get caught all of your cash falls with it.
Trade limit
The most you can use in one round: the higher of your level limit or a share of your cash. It is $1,000 at Lv.1 and grows by 22% per level.
% of limit
The share of your Trade limit you use this round. Choose 10%, 25%, 50% or 100%.
Fees
The cost taken when you buy. It is 0.3% of the amount at 1x and 0.2% more for every step up in multiple.
Rich Tax
A tax on profits once your cash reaches $10M. It starts at 5% and goes up to 65%.
Liquidity Cap
Normal buys cap at $10T per round. Max Buy is the exception.
Base price · +30% line
The base price is the stock’s reference price, shown as a gray dashed line. The yellow dashed line is the daily limit 30% above it. Meme Stocks and Global Giants have no cap, so for them it is only a +30% guide line. Your profit and loss count from your buy price, not the base price.
Dip buy · FOMO buy
Buying while the chart is 4% below the base price is a dip buy, and the crash comes a bit later. Buying 5% above it is a FOMO buy, and the crash comes a bit sooner.
Early protection
In your first 5 real rounds, no crash comes before +20%. Over the next 5 rounds this protection fades out.
Re-entry
After you sell, if the volume signal has not shown yet, you can go back in once with the same amount. You have to sell that part again too.

How rounds flow, and duels

Win streak · Fever
How many rounds in a row ended in a take profit. A bonus starts at 3 in a row, and at 5 you hit Fever, which doubles the bonus rate.
Market mood
The feel of the market, which changes every 5 rounds. There are 8 of them, such as Normal Market, Bull Run and Bear Market, shown in the chip above the chart.
Boss Fight
A duel every 10 rounds. No money is used: you read the boss’s rule and strike while the real attack meter fills.
Bounty
The money you get for defeating a boss: 35% of your level Trade limit (1x) for a new Boss Book entry, 20% for a rematch.

Debt and assets

Credit Grade
AAA is the best and D the worst. A loan drops it by 4 grades, and paying off all your debt raises it by 1.
Loan · Debt
From Lv.10 you can borrow at the Bank. Debt grows by 40–60% interest every round.
Auto Repay
The rule that 30% of every take-profit gain pays down your debt automatically.
Default
When your debt is over the limit and your cash cannot cover it. Your gear, consumables and investments are seized.
Declare bankruptcy
What happens when you go broke while in debt. The debt is wiped, but your gear, consumables and investments are seized and your credit drops to D.
Restart Fund
Money you get from Start fresh or bankruptcy. It is interest-free debt, paid back bit by bit from take-profit gains.
Net Worth · Total assets
Net worth is cash plus investments, minus debt and the Restart Fund. Total assets is net worth plus the asset ladder steps you have bought.
Patience
The average play time your invested money has stayed in. The longer it stays, the higher the rate and cap; every 10x+ or Max Buy round halves it.

Reading the screen, and growth

Order book · Buy power
The order book shows the prices people want to buy at and sell at. Buy power over 100% means buyers are stronger. Turn on 📊 Pro View to see them.
Moving average
The smooth line across the chart. It is the average close of the last 5 candles; when the price sits above it, the price is higher than its recent average.
Bond
How close you are with a partner. It grows +1 per round, +1 for a take profit and +3 for a boss defeat; at 50 the partner becomes an Adult, at 450 a Legend.
SP
Points for upgrading skills. They come slowly from levels, achievements, missions, the Boss Book, the collection and retirement.
Ranks
Titles set by your total XP. There are 25, from Newbie to The Universe Itself, and from Full-time Trader on each rank is split into Ⅰ, Ⅱ and Ⅲ.
Retire · Rebirth
Once your net worth passes the bar, you reset your money and investments and start a new run. Level, skills and gear stay, and you get a permanent bonus.

Stock types

🏛️ Blue Chip
Stocks of large, steady companies. At −45% when caught they lose the least, but they also rise the least.
🔥 Hype Stocks
Stocks that move together on a trending story. In Hype Season their profits grow by 50%.
🐸 Meme Stocks
Stocks with very big swings. They have no daily cap and lose 92% when caught.
🧬 Biotech
Stocks that jump or drop on a single trial result. They lose 80% when caught.
🟡 Penny Stocks
Stocks with a very low price per share. Prices move in the smallest steps, so in percent terms they swing the most.
🎉 IPOs
Companies that just entered the market. Like a first trading day, they swing hard and their crash tends to come early.
🌐 Global Giants
Stocks shown in dollars with no daily cap. They lose 60% when caught.
🎭 Pump Stocks
Stocks someone pushes up on purpose. Their volume signal comes 0.12s earlier. They lose 92% when caught.

All company and stock names in the game are made up. None of them are real companies.

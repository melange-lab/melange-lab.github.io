FAQ

Peakopia guide · as of v3.6.0

Answers to the questions we get most. For anything not covered here, write to melange.labb@gmail.com.

Where is my progress saved?

Only in this device’s browser storage. There is no sign-up, and melange lab has no server that receives game progress. That is why your progress does not show up on other devices.

Can my progress be deleted?

Yes. Clearing site data in your browser deletes it too. In browsers that block saving, such as private mode, it is gone when you close the window, and the game shows a warning in that case. On iPhone, Safari may delete it after a long time without use unless you add the game to your home screen. Save a backup code in your notes now and then.

How do backup codes work?

Tap ‘💾 Copy Backup Code’ in Settings to copy a code that starts with SHG1:. Paste it somewhere safe, such as a notes app. To load it, paste everything from SHG1: to the end into ‘📥 Load Backup’. Your current progress is overwritten. Anyone with the code can load that progress, so keep it safe.

How do I move to a new phone?

  1. On your current device, tap Settings › ‘💾 Copy Backup Code’.
  2. Paste the code somewhere you can also open on the new device, such as a chat with yourself or a notes app.
  3. On the new device, open the game and paste the code into Settings › ‘📥 Load Backup’.
  4. When the game reloads, you can carry on.

Can I compare progress with friends?

Yes, by swapping codes. In ‘💰 Friends’ Net Worth’ on the Account tab, send your code that starts with SHGA-, and paste your friends’ codes to see a net-worth ranking. Daily Challenge scores compete in the Friend League through SHG- codes. Nothing goes through a server; codes go only through the app you choose.

Can I play without internet?

After you open the game once, your browser keeps the game files, so it runs without internet. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app. Features that need the internet, such as ads, only work when you are online.

Does it cost real money? Can I cash out?

No. All money in the game is virtual. There are no purchases, you cannot cash out, and you cannot trade items with other people. All companies, stocks and news in the game are made up.

Will it help me invest for real?

This game is not investment advice. As you level up, Investing notes unlock with short explanations of basics like compound growth, diversification and volatility, but the game’s rules and results have nothing to do with real markets. Do not judge real investments by your game results.

What happens if I run out of money?

If you have no debt, you can start fresh. On the broke screen you can also get a $3,000 comeback bonus once a day and trade again right away. If you have so much debt that the bonus would be seized at once, it is not offered. If you still have debt, you declare bankruptcy and your gear, consumables and investments are seized. Level, skills, achievements, collection and partners stay.

Are there ads?

Not right now. To keep the game free, we plan to turn on Google AdSense ads once AdSense approves the site. When they are on, rewarded ads play only when you tap their button. A full-screen ad between rounds appears at most once every 30 rounds, and only after a round ends. You can play to the end without watching any ads. If ads are off or blocked, you get the comeback bonus without an ad.

Once ads are on, in the EEA, the UK and some other regions, Google shows a consent window, and you can change your choice in ‘🛡️ Privacy & cookie settings’ in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which languages can I play in?

Korean and English. This English version is called Peakopia. The Korean version, 떡상 계좌 키우기, opens at the site root. The English version shows money in dollars, with rising candles in green and falling candles in red. In the Korean version, rising is red and falling is blue.

Is there an Android app?

It is in preparation. Once it is out, the Android app from Google Play will open this web game full-screen in your device’s browser (usually Chrome). The game and the way progress is saved are the same as on the web.

How do I use it like an app on iPhone?

Open the game in Safari, tap the Share button and choose ‘Add to Home Screen’. Opened from the home-screen icon, it runs full-screen, and you worry less about progress being cleared after a long break.

I want to start over from scratch.

Settings › Data › ‘Reset All Progress’ deletes everything, including ranks, skills and achievements. It cannot be undone, so copy a backup code first. Once your net worth passes $1B (then $10B, $100B…), you can also retire instead: it keeps your level and gear and only restarts your money.

How is my nickname chosen?

At first you get a random nickname. Your nickname goes into friend codes and shared results, so do not use your real name or contact details.

Is there an age limit?

This game deals with virtual investing and is not made mainly for children under 14. If a child plays, a parent or guardian should check it together with them.

I found a bug, or I have a question.

Write to melange.labb@gmail.com. Including the version line at the bottom of Settings (for example, v3.6.0 · …) makes it easier to find the cause. You do not need to send any personal information.

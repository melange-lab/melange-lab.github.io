About

melange lab · Operator of Peakopia

melange lab is a small indie game studio. We make HTML5 games that you play right in your browser. We like games that open without an install and can be played in short sessions.

What we make

The game we run now is Peakopia. Its Korean version is called 떡상 계좌 키우기. It is a stock-timing game where you read the chart and sell near the top, using virtual money. It is free to play on the web. An Android app for Google Play is in preparation.

The rules and how to grow are in the game guide.

How we make games

  • Virtual money only. We do not sell in-game money, and it cannot be cashed out. Game results have nothing to do with real money.
  • A game about reading the chart. We avoid effects and wording that leave things to luck. In a normal round, there is always a readable signal before a crash.
  • Rewards are shown up front. The Daily Gift, treasure chests and mission rewards are fixed, and you can see what they are before you claim them. There are no random draws.
  • No pressure. We do not use countdowns that push you to buy or watch ads, or notification badges that call you back.
  • Risky choices, explained calmly. Leverage, Max Buy and loans show their downside next to their upside. You level up just the same at 1x.
  • No personal data collected. There is no sign-up, and progress is saved only on your device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • No real companies. Every company, stock, news item and chat message in the game is made up.

Credits

  • Character, icon and background art: made by the developer with an image generation tool (Higgsfield).
  • Fonts: Noto Sans KR, Inter and Gasoek One, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and bundled with the game files.
  • Sound effects and music: synthesized inside the game. No outside audio is used.

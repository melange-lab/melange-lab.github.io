Privacy Policy

Peakopia · Operated by melange lab

Effective date: November 19, 2026

In short
  • No account or sign-up. melange lab does not collect personal information.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. melange lab has no server that receives game data.
  • We do not use analytics.
  • All money in the game is virtual. There are no purchases and no cash-out.
  • Google AdSense ads may be shown on the website and in the Android app, and Google may use cookies and similar technologies for those ads.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the game Peakopia (Korean title: 떡상 계좌 키우기), operated by melange lab, on its website (https://melange-lab.github.io) and in the Android app distributed through Google Play. The Android app opens the same web game full-screen in your device's browser (usually Chrome).

If you play on another game site (for example, CrazyGames), that site's privacy policy applies there, and that site provides the ads.

2. Information we collect

None. melange lab does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, address, location, contacts or photos. There are no purchases, so we receive no payment information. The game does not use location, the camera, the microphone or your contacts.

Google and GitHub, described in sections 4 and 5, are not places we send your data to. They communicate with your device directly and handle information under their own policies.

3. Data stored on your device (game progress)

So you can keep playing, the following is saved in your browser's storage (localStorage) on your device. The Android app saves it the same way, in the storage of the browser the app opens.

  • Game progress: virtual assets, level, skills, achievements, collection, missions and similar
  • Game settings such as sound
  • Your nickname and Friend League scores (you start with a random nickname such as "Trader1234")
  • The last 10 game error messages (kept on your device only and never included in the backup code)

This data is never sent to a melange lab server, and melange lab cannot see it. To work offline, the game also caches its own files (images, fonts and so on) on your device. In the Android app, the game asks the browser to keep its storage persistent so your progress is not cleared automatically when space runs low (no permission prompt is shown).

Backup code

Tapping "Copy Backup Code" in Settings copies a code containing your game progress (including your nickname) to your clipboard. Where you keep it is up to you; melange lab never receives it. Anyone with the code can load that progress, so keep it to yourself.

Share result and friend codes

When you use "Share result" or a Friend League code, a short text with your nickname and score goes to your device's share sheet or clipboard. It is sent only to the app you choose (a messenger, for example) and does not pass through melange lab. Don't use your real name or contact details as your nickname.

4. Ads (Google AdSense)

To keep the game free, Google AdSense ads may be shown on the website and in the Android app: ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and occasional ads between rounds.

When ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies, device or browser identifiers, and your IP address to choose ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Google receives this information directly; it is not passed to melange lab. melange lab only sees aggregated reports such as ad earnings.

  • Personalized ads are based on things like your past visits to this and other websites.
  • Non-personalized ads are based on general information such as the screen you are viewing and your approximate location (from your IP address). Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or similar technologies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings. You can opt out of other vendors' cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info. For details, see how Google uses information for advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, Google shows a consent message. You can change your choice later under "Privacy & cookie settings" in the game's Settings.

5. Other services

  • GitHub Pages (website hosting): the game files are served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open the game on the web or in the app, GitHub may log your IP address and similar request data for hosting and security. melange lab does not receive these visitor logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google Play (app distribution): Google Play handles installing and updating the Android app, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may show melange lab statistics such as install counts, device types and app errors in a form that does not identify you.
  • Your device's browser: the Android app opens the game in your device's browser (usually Chrome). Information the browser itself handles is covered by that browser's policy.

We do not use external font servers, analytics tools or social media tracking code. The fonts are included in the game files.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

melange lab does not use cookies to track you. The game uses only your browser's storage (localStorage) to save your progress, and that data stays on your device. When ads are on, Google may set and use advertising cookies (see section 4).

How to refuse them:

  • Chrome on Android: ⋮ menu › Settings › Site settings › Third-party cookies, then block
  • Safari on iPhone: Settings › Safari, then turn on "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking" or "Block All Cookies"
  • To turn off personalized ads only: Google Ad Settings

If you block all cookies and site data, the game may not be able to save your progress. In that case, copy your backup code.

7. Retention and deletion

melange lab keeps no personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data on your device stays there until you delete it. To delete it:

  • In the game: Settings › Data › "Reset All Progress"
  • On the web: clear the site data (cookies and site data) for melange-lab.github.io in your browser settings
  • Android app: uninstall the app. Because the app uses your browser's storage (usually Chrome), your progress may remain after uninstalling. If so, go to Chrome › Settings › Site settings › All sites › melange-lab.github.io and clear its data.
  • Delete any backup codes you copied from wherever you saved them.

Browsers may also clear data for sites you haven't used in a while (Safari on iPhone, for example).

8. Children

This game is about virtual investing and is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). melange lab does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a child plays, we ask a parent or guardian to check the game with them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, the UK, Korea or some US states), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because melange lab holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or transfer. You can view and delete the game data on your device at any time (section 7). melange lab does not sell personal information. Ad data that Google processes can be managed in Google Ad Settings and your Google Account. For any other questions, use the contact in section 11.

10. Security

We collect and store no personal information, so there is nothing of that kind for us to secure. The game website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
